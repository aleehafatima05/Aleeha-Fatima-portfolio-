My story

Curiosity into capability.

I am Aleeha Fatima, a BS Artificial Intelligence student at Bahauddin Zakariya University (BZU), Multan, Pakistan, and founder of AF Techworks.

I am interested in artificial intelligence, programming, web development and practical digital solutions. I enjoy exploring how technology works, researching ideas and building projects that help me apply what I learn.

I value independent thinking, system-level understanding, clear communication and continuous improvement. My professional journey is developing through education, experimentation and project work.
