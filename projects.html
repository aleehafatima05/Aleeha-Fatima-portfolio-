Smart Buy PK

Smart Buy PK is an independent research and technology project developed by Aleeha Fatima to help users make more informed product-purchasing decisions.

  • Product research and exploration.
  • Product quality and price comparisons.
  • Budget-aware product exploration.
  • Product availability sources and purchasing guidance.
  • Social media reviews and opinions.
  • Relevant source information, including medical perspectives where applicable.

This portfolio does not claim real-time price tracking; see the live site for its current features.
